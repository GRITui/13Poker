How to play

Everyone gets 13 cards from one 52-card deck (no jokers, no wild cards). Split yours into three poker hands:

  • Back: 5 cards, your strongest hand
  • Middle: 5 cards, must be higher than the Front
  • Front: 3 cards, your weakest hand

The Back must be strictly higher than the Middle, and the Middle strictly higher than the Front. Otherwise the hand is fouled. The game won't let you set a fouled hand.

Hand ranks

Standard poker, high to low: straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, high card. Ace is high, and also low in A-2-3-4-5 (the lowest straight). No wrap-around (Q-K-A-2-3 is not a straight). Suits never break ties.

In the 3-card Front, straights and flushes don't count: only three of a kind, a pair or high card. 7♥ 8♥ 9♥ is just 9-high.

Scoring

Your Front, Middle and Back are compared with the same row of each opponent. You win 1 unit for each row you win and lose 1 for each row you lose. Ties pay nothing.

Scoop ×2: win all three rows (Front, Middle and Back) against one player and your row points double, 3 × 2 = 6. A tied row means no scoop.

A fouled hand loses all three rows to every valid hand, so it is scooped and pays 6. Two fouled hands score 0 against each other.

Optional rules

Royalties (off by default): Front trips 3; Middle full house 2, quads 8, straight flush 10; Back quads 4, straight flush 5.

Naturals (off by default): Dragon (A to K, one of each rank) 13, Six pairs 3, Three flushes 3, Three straights 3. A declared natural is paid instead of comparing hands.

Controls

By default your 13 cards are dealt straight into the rows at random, usually as a fouled hand. Swap cards between rows until the Back beats the Middle and the Middle beats the Front. Tap a card, then tap a card in another row to swap them, or drag one card onto another.

The rules follow the Washington State Gambling Commission "Thirteen Card Poker (Chinese Poker)" sheet as reported by search snippets, plus common conventions where the sheet is silent. See RULES.md.
